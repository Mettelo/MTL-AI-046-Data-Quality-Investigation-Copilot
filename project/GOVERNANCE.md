# Governance

## Simulation and accountability

This is an educational research assignment with fictional stakeholders and operational dependencies. Do not claim real-client work, endorsement, operational deployment, health conclusions or realised benefits. Actual members/reviewers must be named separately from simulated roles.

Data Operations Lead owns the simulated scope/priority policy. Governance owns rights, source freeze and retention. Engineering owns reproducible facts. AI engineering owns generation and model inventory. Assurance independently checks evidence. The simulated Mettelo panel records programme acceptance.

## Gates

| Gate | Evidence / approval |
| --- | --- |
| G1 week 2 | R1–R8 scope, source notices/rights decision, actual acquisition/profile, local inference smoke test, named owners/reviewers and risk log. No source substitution without review. |
| G2 week 4 | Parser and scenario freeze, baseline profile, rule configuration, lineage, evidence schemas, B0 and independent calculations. |
| G3 week 6 | Frozen model/prompt/retrieval, held-out inventory, raw metrics, guard and human review evidence; decide remediation when targets fail. |
| G4 week 8 | D1–D12, SC1–SC5, AC/DT/UAT results, clean rerun, final index and zero critical/high defects. |

Each approval: gate, specification commit, decision, actual approver, simulated role, date, evidence, conditions and follow-up owner. A heading saying “approved” is not evidence.

## Risk register requirements

Track risk ID, trigger, consequence, likelihood/impact, owner, prevention, detection, contingency, review date and residual risk. Minimum risks: source licensing conflict, upstream byte changes, sentinel misuse, false causal diagnosis, incomplete lineage, held-out leakage, prompt injection, model hallucination, hardware/timeouts, lost review state and paid dependencies.

All model suggestions are untrusted until checked. Evidence citations alone do not establish semantic support. Human review checks counts, causal wording, impact and safe next action. No model ability to repair data or execute SQL/shell is in scope.

## Source and model rights

Retain both UCI CC BY and research-only statements; keep this assignment educational/research and avoid commercial deployment without clarification. Do not apply a blanket project license to third-party data/models. Record model card, license, artifact digest, quantisation and runtime. No paid provider keys or participant personal data are needed.

## Data handling

Keep immutable source and model artifacts outside Git, with hashes, restore instructions and recoverable copy. Repository may contain schemas, manifests, small authored synthetic fixtures and derived aggregate results consistent with source restrictions. Label every fixture and public sample; do not commit real personal/company data, secrets, large model weights or confidential logs. Use localhost binding by default. Escape untrusted interface content.

At kickoff specify storage owner, reviewer access, retention period, deletion method and backup schedule according to cohort policy. If no policy is supplied, propose a project retention period and record approval; do not invent a mandatory institutional policy.

## Change and model release control

Log changed requirement/source/rule/prompt/model/reference, rationale, reviewer, affected D/AC/DT, version and retest scope. New source bytes require a new freeze. Changed model/prompt after holdout exposure requires fresh blind cases for a new unbiased headline result. Version incident schemas and keep older exports readable.

## Human authority and incident escalation

Reviewer chooses approve, reject or needs-more-evidence, assigns a human action owner and records rationale. Generation is never approval. Unsupported critical claims block export as approved. Escalate an unresolved DQ issue to the simulated data owner with run/evidence IDs; no production notification is sent by the product.

All benefits, capacities, severity thresholds and dependent assets in this pack are simulation assumptions unless attributed. A prototype passing this assignment is not production approval.
